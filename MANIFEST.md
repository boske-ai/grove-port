# Boske Community — manifest

*For contributors and agents working in this repo.*

## Purpose

Public MIT home of **Grove Port** (portable AI workspace format + adapters + CLI). Sibling Community OSS: **[Boske Pulse](https://github.com/boske-ai/boske-pulse)**.

The **Boske** product app, cloud, and signing keys stay proprietary.

## Layout

| Path | Role |

| [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) | MIT |

## Agent guardrails

1. Plan non-trivial Grove Port work under `grove-port/docs/work/active/`.
2. Do not commit secrets, real user exports, or `*.grove-port` fixtures with personal data.
3. Keep the public surface focused on Grove Port — do not expand this hub into a product roadmap dump.
4. Pulse lives in its own public repo; do not vendor it here.
